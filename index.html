    Crochet your own Clawd

    A written crochet pattern asks you to picture a shape you have never seen. This builds it in 3D from the instructions, one round at a time. Free, no account, runs in this browser.

    How to use it

    1. Read the round you are on. One instruction at a time, with the stitch count you should end on.
    2. Check it against the model. Grey is what you have already worked. The round you are on right now is yellow.
    3. Hit Next when that round is done. Arrow keys work too, and the bars at the top jump to any step.

    The model shows stitch placement and shape, not yarn texture.
